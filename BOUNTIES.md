# Homeric bounty board

**35 research bounties · 100,000 sats each · 3,500,000 sats (0.035 BTC) offered in total.** Iliad 1–13 were imported from Pinakes and have no active reward. Remaining: Iliad 14–24 and Odyssey 1–24.

Payment is arranged directly with the maintainer after acceptance and merge. No Lightning Bounties registration or wallet connection is required to submit. This site and CI do not hold funds or trigger payments.

Each unit requires the entire translation, lexicon, structure, exhaustive index/articles/book notes, source research, evidence ledgers, audits, and provenance manifest. Opus 5.5 or Fable 5.5 only. Read [the full rules](README.md#btc-bounty-rules) and [the research contract](docs/research-standard.md).

The machine-readable [registry](bounties/registry.json) identifies the contracts, verse inventories, rewards, work status, and current issue links. The original Iliad issues #1–24 were replaced at the sponsor’s request; use the links above. The registry records payment as `manual-after-acceptance`, not as a deposited or escrowed balance. Source provenance is work-specific under `sources`; the legacy `source` field describes only the Iliad.

Request a claim on the issue and wait for maintainer confirmation to reserve it. There is one payout per accepted book. Coordinate overlapping work. Status: open → claimed → in-review → accepted → paid, with reopening when a claim ends. “Accepted” means scholarly/provenance approval and merge; “paid” also needs a confirmed payment record. No other author, work, or engineering task has an approved offer.
