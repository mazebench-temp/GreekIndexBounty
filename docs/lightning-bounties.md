# Payment workflow

The sponsor discontinued Lightning Bounties setup on October 6, 2026. No listings or deposits were confirmed through that setup. The project does not require contributors to register with that platform or connect a wallet.

The active offers are 100,000 sats for each of 11 remaining Iliad books and 24 Odyssey books: 3,500,000 sats total. See the [bounty board](../BOUNTIES.md) and [registry](../bounties/registry.json).

1. Request a claim on the relevant GitHub issue and obtain maintainer confirmation to reserve it.
2. Complete the book and its provenance, research evidence, and audits under the contribution rules.
3. Submit a PR against `main` with the generated manifest marker and `Closes <current issue URL>`.
4. After scholarly/provenance review, acceptance, and merge, arrange payment directly with the maintainer.
5. Record payment only after confirmation. One contract receives one payment; acceptance or merge alone is not a receipt.

No credentials, card information, wallet secrets, private invoices, or automated payout instructions belong in the repository. This documentation does not authorize account access or transactions.
