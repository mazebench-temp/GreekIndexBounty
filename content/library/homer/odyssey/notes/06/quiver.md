---
summary: The quiver is named with the bow as a thing the Phaeacians do not care for.
grc: [φαρέτρη]
---

“For the Phaeacians do not care for the bow or the quiver” (βιὸς οὐδὲ φαρέτρη, 6.270; see [[bow]] and [[ships]]). The pair names the archer's equipment. The Phaeacians' care is for masts, oars and ships instead (6.271–272; see [[mast]] and [[oars]]).
