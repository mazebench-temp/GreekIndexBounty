---
summary: In Nausicaa's description of the city, “the curving ships” are drawn up along the road into the harbor (6.264).
---

Nausicaa describes the approach to the city: a high wall, a fine harbor on each side, “and a narrow entrance; and along the road the curving ships / are drawn up; for all have a ship-shed, each man his own” (6.262–265). The ships line the way that Odysseus will walk. The meaning of ἀμφιέλισσα is disputed (see the entry). See [[ships]], [[epistion]], [[scheria]] and [[phaeacians]].
