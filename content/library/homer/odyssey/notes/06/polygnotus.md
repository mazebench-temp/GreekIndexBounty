---
summary: Polygnotus painted Odysseus coming upon Nausicaa and the washing girls by the river, a painting that Pausanias saw at the Propylaea of the Athenian Acropolis.
---

Pausanias names the painting among those in the building on the left of the Propylaea: Polygnotus painted Achilles among the maidens on Scyros, and “he also painted, by the river, Odysseus coming upon the girls who were washing with Nausicaa, in the same way as Homer composed it” (1.22.6). The painting showed the scene of 6.127–138. See [[nausicaa-in-greek-art]].
