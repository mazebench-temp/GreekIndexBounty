---
summary: Nausicaa says that the Phaeacians care for “masts and the oars of ships and balanced ships” instead of bows and quivers (6.271).
grc: ["νῆες ἐῖσαι"]
---

“For the Phaeacians do not care for the bow or the quiver, / but for masts and the oars of ships and balanced ships, / glorying in which they cross the gray sea” (6.270–272; [[gray-sea]]). The verse ends with νῆες ἐῖσαι. Nausicaa defines her people by their ships, not by weapons. The contrast with the bow is pointed for a reader who knows the end of the poem, but Nausicaa does not know who the stranger is. See [[phaeacians]], [[ships]] and [[famous-for-ships]].
