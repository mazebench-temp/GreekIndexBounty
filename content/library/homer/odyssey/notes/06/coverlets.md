---
summary: The glossy coverlets of the house are part of the laundry that Athena tells Nausicaa to take to the river.
---

Athena's list of the laundry ends with them: the wagon is to carry “the sashes and the robes and the glossy coverlets” (ζῶστρά τε καὶ πέπλους καὶ ῥήγεα σιγαλόεντα, 6.38; see [[clothes]] and [[wagon]]). LSJ takes ῥήγεα here as probably garments, since they are washed with sashes and robes. Elsewhere the same phrase ῥήγεα σιγαλόεντα names the coverlets of a bed (11.189, 19.318, 23.180). The household linen and the household's clothes go to the washing pools together (see [[washing-clothes]]).
