summary: The boars of Book 6 are Artemis' quarry in the simile of the goddess among her nymphs.
grc: [κάπροισι]
en: [wild boars]
---

Artemis ranges the mountains “delighting in wild boars and swift deer” (τερπομένη κάπροισι καὶ ὠκείῃς ἐλάφοισι, 6.104; see [[artemis-among-her-nymphs]] and [[deer]]). The mountains are Taygetus and Erymanthus (6.103; see [[taygetus]] and [[erymanthus]]). Here κάπρος stands alone, without σῦς (compare ἢ συσὶ κάπροισιν, Iliad 5.783). The scholia explain the choice of animals. Erymanthus is “a mountain of Arcadia, where many boars and deer are said to live” (B.P.Q.), and “these places abound in boars and deer; he did not say lions, since these animals are not bred there, as Aristotle says” (Dindorf p. 302; Dindorf's note refers to Aristotle, *History of Animals* 6.31; see [[aristotle-lions-of-europe]]).
