---
summary: The boars of Book 6 are Artemis' quarry in the simile of the goddess among her nymphs.
grc: [κάπροισι]
en: [wild boars]
---

Artemis ranges the mountains “delighting in wild boars and swift deer” (τερπομένη κάπροισι καὶ ὠκείῃς ἐλάφοισι, 6.104; see [[artemis-among-her-nymphs]] and [[deer]]). The mountains are Taygetus and Erymanthus (6.103; see [[taygetus]] and [[erymanthus]]). Here κάπρος stands alone, without σῦς (compare ἢ συσὶ κάπροισιν, Iliad 5.783). A scholion on 6.103–104 explains that Homer gives the goddess boars and deer and not lions to hunt, and cites Aristotle on the absence of lions in that part of Europe (see [[aristotle-lions-of-europe]]).
