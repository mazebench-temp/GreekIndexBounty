---
summary: The Phaeacians “cross the gray sea”, glorying in their ships (6.272).
grc: [πολιὴν]
---

“Glorying in which they cross the gray sea” (ᾗσιν ἀγαλλόμενοι πολιὴν περόωσι θάλασσαν, 6.272). The adjective and the noun stand apart, with the verb between them, and the verse ends with θάλασσαν. The line closes Nausicaa's picture of a people of ships (6.270–272; [[balanced-ships]]). See [[sea]] and [[phaeacians]].
