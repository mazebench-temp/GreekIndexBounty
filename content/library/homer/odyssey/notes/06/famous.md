---
summary: κλειτός occurs once in Book 6. Alcinous goes out to join “the famous kings” in council (6.54). The κλυτός of 6.326 belongs to the separate entry klytos.
except: [6.326]
---

Nausicaa meets her father at the door “as he went to join the famous kings / in council, where the noble Phaeacians had summoned him” (μετὰ κλειτοὺς βασιλῆας, 6.54–55). The kings are the leaders of the Phaeacians. In Book 8 Alcinous says that twelve kings rule in the land and that he is the thirteenth (8.390–391; [[kings]]). The epithet gives the council the dignity of the heroic assemblies of the Iliad, but its business on Scheria is peaceful. See [[alcinous]] and [[phaeacians]].

The other words for fame in Book 6 are forms of κλυτός, “famous, glorious” (6.36, 6.58, 6.321, 6.326; [[klytos]]).
