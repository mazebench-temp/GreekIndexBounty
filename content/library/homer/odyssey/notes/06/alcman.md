---
summary: A scholion on 6.244 says that Alcman adapted Nausicaa's wish for a husband, putting into the mouths of girls the words “Father Zeus, if only he were my husband”. The adaptation made Aristarchus hesitate to reject the line.
---

The scholion in mss. H and Q on 6.244–245 reports that Aristarchus athetized both lines of Nausicaa's wish, “but hesitates about the first, since Alcman also adapted it, bringing on girls who say ‘Ζεῦ πάτερ, αἲ γὰρ ἐμὸς πόσις εἴη’”, “Father Zeus, if only he were my husband” (Dindorf p. 314). Alcman's line keeps Homer's wish formula αἲ γάρ and the word πόσις, “husband”, and turns the wish into a prayer to Zeus. The quotation is the only Book 6 evidence for Alcman, and its place among his fragments was not checked for this entry. See [[nausicaas-wish-244-245]].
