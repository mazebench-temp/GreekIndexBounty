---
summary: Athena's grove, where Odysseus prays at sunset, is “holy to Athena” (ἱρὸν Ἀθηναίης, 6.322), with the Ionic form ἱρός.
grc: [ἱρὸν]
---

“And the sun set, and they came to the famous grove, / holy to Athena, where brilliant Odysseus sat down” (6.321–322; [[klytos]], [[grove-of-athena]]). ἱρόν is the Ionic form of ἱερόν. The grove is the place that Nausicaa described: “a splendid grove of Athena near the road”, of poplars, with a spring and a meadow, and her father's estate (6.291–294; [[temenos]]). In the holy grove of the goddess Odysseus prays to her (6.323–327; [[atrytone]], [[prayer-type-scene]]).
