---
summary: Lucretius renders Homer's description of Olympus (6.42–46) point by point for the quiet dwellings of the Epicurean gods (De rerum natura 3.18–24).
---

Homer's Olympus is shaken by no winds, wet by no rain and touched by no snow, and a cloudless sky with a white gleam spreads over it (6.42–46). In the proem to his third book, Lucretius sees “the divinity of the gods and their quiet dwellings, which no winds shake, no clouds sprinkle with rain, no snow … violates, but an always cloudless sky covers them and laughs with light spread wide” (3.18–22).
