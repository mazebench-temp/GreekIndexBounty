---
summary: Pliny identifies Homer's Scheria with Corcyra and names the palm of Delos among the oldest trees still shown.
---

- **Scheria.** Corcyra was “called Scheria and Phaeacia by Homer, and Drepane by Callimachus too” (*Natural History* 4.52). See [[corcyra-and-the-phaeacians]].
- **The palm of Delos.** In a list of ancient trees: “and the palm of Delos is seen, from the time of that same god”, that is, from the birth of Apollo (16.240). Odysseus saw a young palm by Apollo's altar on Delos (6.162–163). See [[the-palm-of-delos]].
