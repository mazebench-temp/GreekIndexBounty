---
summary: Nausithous builds temples of the gods when he founds the Phaeacian city on Scheria.
grc: [νηούς]
en: [temples]
---

The founding of the city is told in one sentence. Nausithous led the Phaeacians from Hypereia, settled them in Scheria, “and drove a wall around the city, and built houses, and made temples of the gods, and divided the plowlands” (6.9–10; see [[nausithous]], [[the-phaeacians-leave-hypereia]] and [[scheria]]). Temples stand in the list of the founder's works with the wall, the houses and the division of land. The plural νηούς, “temples”, must be kept apart from νηός, “of a ship”, at 6.278 (see [[ships]]). The one sacred building named in Nausicaa's description of the city is the Posideion at the place of assembly, which she does not call a νηός (6.266; see [[posideion]]).
