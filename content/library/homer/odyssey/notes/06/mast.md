---
summary: The Phaeacians care for “masts and the oars of ships”, not for bows. In Book 6 ἱστοί are masts only.
grc: [ἱστοί]
en: [masts]
---

“For the Phaeacians do not care for the bow or the quiver, but for masts and the oars of ships and balanced ships, glorying in which they cross the gray sea” (6.270–272; see [[ships]] and [[oars]]). ἱστός is both the mast and the upright [[loom]]. Book 6 has no loom: the queen spins (6.53, 6.306; see [[distaff]]), and the only ἱστοί are the ships' masts. In Book 7 the Phaeacian women “weave at the looms” (ἱστοὺς ὑφόωσι, 7.105), and the same word stands for the other craft for which the Phaeacians are famous (7.108–111).
