---
summary: Sophocles staged the meeting of Odysseus and Nausicaa in a lost play, the Nausicaa or Plyntriai (“The Washerwomen”), and he is said to have played the ball game himself.
---

- The play, its two titles, its three fragments, and the story that Sophocles played ball as Nausicaa (Athenaeus 1.20f; Eustathius on 6.115, p. 241): [[sophocles-nausicaa]].
- **The chorus.** The plural title names washerwomen. They are taken to be Nausicaa's handmaids, who tread the clothes in the washing trenches and then play ball with her (6.85–101; see [[handmaids-of-nausicaa]]).
