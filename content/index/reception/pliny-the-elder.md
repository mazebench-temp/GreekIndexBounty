title: Pliny the Elder

tags: [scholarship]
summary: Roman officer and writer of the first century CE, author of the Natural History in thirty-seven books, an encyclopedia of the world from astronomy and geography to plants, animals, medicine and art. He died in the eruption of Vesuvius in 79 CE.
aliases: [Gaius Plinius Secundus, Natural History, Naturalis Historia]
---

The *Natural History* collects facts from hundreds of earlier writers, and it often cites Homer as a witness to places and things. Its books on geography (3–6) name the Homeric places of the coasts and islands, and its books on trees (12–17) record famous old trees.
