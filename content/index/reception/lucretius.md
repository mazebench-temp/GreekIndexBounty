summary: Roman poet of the first century BCE, author of De rerum natura (“On the Nature of Things”), a poem in six books that sets out the physics and ethics of Epicurus in Latin hexameters.
aliases: [Titus Lucretius Carus, De rerum natura, On the Nature of Things]
---

Lucretius praises Epicurus as the man who freed humanity from the fear of the gods and of death. His gods live apart in untroubled calm and take no part in the world. He writes in the epic hexameter, and he adapts Homer's language and images to Epicurean doctrine.
