---
title: Alcman
greek: Ἀλκμάν
kind: author
tags: [art, music]
summary: Lyric poet of Sparta in the seventh century BCE, best known for songs composed for choruses of girls. A scholion on the Odyssey reports that he adapted a line of Nausicaa's for such a chorus.
aliases: [Alcman of Sparta]
---

Alcman wrote in the Doric dialect of Laconia, and his poems survive only in quotations and papyri. His songs for choruses of unmarried girls are known as partheneia, “maiden songs”.
