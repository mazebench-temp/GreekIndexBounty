Aristophanes of Byzantium belongs to the line of Alexandrian editors of Homer that runs from [[zenodotus]] to his pupil [[aristarchus]]. The scholia cite his readings as “Aristophanes writes …”, and his pupil Callistratus is cited beside him. He is not the comic poet Aristophanes of Athens.
