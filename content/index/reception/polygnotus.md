---
title: Polygnotus
greek: Πολύγνωτος
kind: author
tags: [art]
summary: Painter from Thasos, active in Athens and Delphi in the fifth century BCE, known for large wall paintings of scenes from the Trojan saga. Pausanias saw his painting of Odysseus and Nausicaa at the entrance to the Athenian Acropolis.
aliases: [Polygnotus of Thasos]
---

Pausanias describes Polygnotus' paintings at length, above all the paintings in the Lesche of the Cnidians at Delphi, where an inscription named him “Polygnotus, a Thasian by birth, son of Aglaophon” (10.27.4). None of his works survives.
