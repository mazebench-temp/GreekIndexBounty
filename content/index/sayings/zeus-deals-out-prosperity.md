**The context.** Odysseus has come out of the bushes naked and crusted with brine, and he has begged Nausicaa for pity (6.149–185; see [[supplication]]). She answers that he seems “neither a base man nor a senseless one” (6.187). The maxim follows at once. Zeus gives ὄλβος, “prosperity, happiness” (see [[olbos]]), to good and bad men “as he wishes”, so the stranger's misery says nothing about his worth. He must bear it: σὲ δὲ χρὴ τετλάμεν ἔμπης, “you must endure them all the same” (6.190). The verb τλῆναι is the root of Odysseus' own epithets, πολύτλας, “much-enduring”, which the narrator gives him in the first verse of the book (6.1).

**The grammar.** Merry and Riddell take 6.188–190 as a parenthesis. The ἐπεί clause of 6.187 finds its main clause only at 6.191–192, “you will not lack clothing, then”. The maxim is a reflection suggested by the stranger's state (note on 6.187, p. 269). The translation notes the same break (TRANSLATION.md, on 6.187). Merry and Riddell say that the point lies in ὅπως ἐθέλῃσιν, “as he wishes”: the gods give good and evil by no rule but their will (note on 6.188–189, p. 269).

**The same thought elsewhere.** The idea that Zeus gives fortune as he wills, without regard to desert, recurs in the Odyssey and the Iliad:

- Helen, to Menelaus and his guests: “the god, Zeus, gives good and evil now to one, now to another; for he can do all things” (4.236–237).
- Eumaeus to the disguised Odysseus: “the god will give one thing and let another be, / as his heart wishes; for he can do all things” (14.444–445).
- Achilles to Priam: two jars stand on the floor of Zeus' house, one of evils and one of good gifts. To some men Zeus gives a mixture, to others only evils (Iliad 24.527–533; see [[gifts-of-the-gods]]).

Zeus himself states a different view at the beginning of the Odyssey. Men blame the gods for their evils, but they suffer beyond their portion through their own recklessness (1.32–34). The two views stand side by side in the poem. Nausicaa uses the first one to explain the stranger's state before she knows who he is.
